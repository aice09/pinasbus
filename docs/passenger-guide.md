# PINASBUS passenger guide

For people booking bus trips and sending Padala on PINASBUS.

## Account

**Create account** asks for your name as on your ID, email, mobile number, and a password of at least 10 characters. Under **Settings** you can set "I usually travel as" (regular, senior citizen, PWD, student or child), change your password (this signs you out everywhere), or delete the account.

## Find a trip

1. On the home page choose **From**, **To** and a date. The lists only offer pairs some bus company actually runs: pick a boarding point and the destinations narrow to those reachable from it.
2. The results show every company's departures. For each one you see:
   - the company and the time the bus leaves *your* stop;
   - the bus class, **With CR / No CR**, and upper and lower berths on sleepers;
   - whether the trip includes a ferry;
   - seats left and the fare for your segment.
3. A trip marked **Indicative time and fare** uses demonstration values, because the company publishes no official schedule we could read. Confirm with the company.

## Choose seats

- Turn the **3D bus** or use the **Seat plan**, the same information as an accessible grid.
- **L** and **U** are lower and upper berths. On the 3D view, **Lift the upper deck** shows the lower berths.
- Coloured seats with a badge are priority seats:

  | Badge | Seat | Who can book it |
  |---|---|---|
  | ♿ (blue) | PWD accessible | passengers with disabilities |
  | SC (amber) | Senior citizen priority | senior citizens |
  | P (purple) | Priority | senior citizens and PWD |

  Each company sets a deadline, for example 2 hours before departure. Priority seats still unsold by then open to everyone. Eligible passengers may always choose a regular seat instead.
- Up to 6 seats per booking. For each seat choose a saved passenger or type a name, and choose the passenger type.
- **Senior citizen, PWD and student fares** need the ID type and the last 4 characters of the ID number. Bring the ID: it is checked at boarding. The discount each company gives is shown next to the type. PINASBUS keeps only the ID type and those 4 characters.
- Change **Pick-up** and **Drop-off** to any stops on that trip; the fare follows the segment.

## Pay

**Pay** holds the seats for 15 minutes and opens PayMongo's checkout. You can pay with GCash, Maya or a card. The total includes an online convenience fee of 5.2% + ₱16 per seat. After paying you land on your ticket (reference **PB…**). It lists every seat, passenger, passenger type and fare. Show it, or just the reference, at the boarding point 30 minutes early.

If payment didn't finish, the ticket says so. The seats free up after 15 minutes.

## Saved passengers, history and booking again

- **Saved passengers** (up to 20): name, mobile, a label such as "Mama", passenger type, and the discount ID type and its last 4 characters. Only you can see them.
- **Trips** lists every booking made while signed in. **Book again** keeps the route and asks for a new date, time and seats.
- **Padala** lists your packages with **Track** and **Send again**.

## Padala

Choose drop-off and claim terminals (only pairs a bus serves are offered). Fill in sender, recipient, contents, weight and declared value; the fee is shown as you type. After paying, drop the package off within 3 days and track it with its **PD…** reference. The public tracking page shows only first names and last initials.

## Refunds

A payment that arrives after your hold expired gets the seats back if they are still free. If they were resold, the ticket says **Paid, but the seats were taken** and the company refunds you. For anything else, use **Contact us** with your reference.

## Baggage

On a paid ticket, open **Register bags**. For each bag, choose its owner, describe it, and give its type and weight. The company's allowance applies: often 20 kg per seat and up to 3 bags per seat. Anything over is charged per kg at the terminal check-in. A bag can't exceed the bus's per-bag limit or the space left in its hold. Each bag gets a tag code (`BG…`). Staff scan it at each step:

> checked in → weighed → tagged → loaded → in transit → unloaded → ready for collection → released

You see each step on the ticket and in **Account → Baggage**, and get a notification when a bag is loaded, ready, released, or has a problem.

## Tracking your bus

**Track this bus** on a paid ticket opens a live map with:
- the bus position;
- speed, average and top speed;
- distance travelled and remaining;
- arrival estimate and next stop;
- ferry status on routes with a crossing.

Positions come from the crew's phone or the bus's GPS. When neither is reporting, the map shows a **simulated** position, clearly labelled, based on the timetable.

## City buses

City routes (for example the EDSA Carousel) are walk-up: you pay on board and can't reserve a seat. Their page shows the line like a train map: every stop in order, your leg highlighted, times, and the bus on the map.

## Transport cards

In **Account → Transport cards**, link a PINASBUS card with its 16-digit number. You can then:
- load it with GCash, Maya or a bank card;
- see its balance, history and the trips paid with it;
- pay fares with it (choose the card under **Pay with**).

Refunds of card-paid tickets go back to the card.

## Notifications, security and help

- The bell shows booking, payment, baggage, ferry, delay and card notifications. Important ones are also emailed.
- **Account → Security** turns on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. It is required for administrator and finance roles.
- **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page emails a reset link that works for 30 minutes, once.
- **Ask PINASBUS** (bottom-right) is an AI assistant. It answers from live schedules and can find trips and fares, check a ticket by reference, or say where your bus is. It can't book or refund, and the trip and ticket pages are authoritative.
