# BikolBus user guide

For passengers and Padala senders.

## Book a seat

1. On the home page choose **From**, **To** and a **Travel date**, then **Find trips**. The lists only offer pairs a bus actually runs: pick a pick-up point and the destinations narrow to the ones reachable from it (and the other way round).
2. The trips list shows each departure's pick-up time, bus class, **With CR / No CR**, upper and lower berths on sleepers, seats left and the fare. Use the date strip to move between days.
3. **Choose seats.** Turn the 3D bus by dragging, zoom with scroll or pinch, and tap seats. On sleeper buses, berths are marked **L** (lower deck) and **U** (upper deck); **Lift the upper deck** shows the lower berths underneath. The **Seat plan** view is the same thing as a keyboard- and screen-reader-friendly grid, with the restroom (CR) drawn where it is on the bus.
4. Under **Who is sitting where**, choose a saved passenger for each seat (signed-in users) or type a name. Empty names use the contact person.
5. Fill in the contact person, email and mobile, then **Pay**. Seats are held for 15 minutes while you pay with GCash, Maya or card on PayMongo.
6. After payment you land on the ticket page (reference **BB…**). Show it, or the reference, with a valid ID at boarding, 30 minutes before departure.

Up to 6 seats per booking. Student, senior citizen and PWD discounts are given only at terminal booths.

## Your account

Create one at **Create account** (name, email, mobile, a password of at least 10 characters).

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Overview | Upcoming trips, Padala on the way, one-tap **Book a route again** for routes you have taken |
| Trips | Every booking made while signed in, upcoming and past, with **Ticket** and **Book again** |
| Padala | Every package sent while signed in, with **Track** and **Send again** |
| Saved passengers | Add, edit and delete family members and travel companions (up to 20). Only you can see them |
| Settings | Change name and mobile, change password (signs out every device), delete the account |

**Book again** keeps the route and opens the trips list so you choose a new date, time and seats. **Send again** prefills the Padala form with the same route, recipient and package.

Deleting your account removes it and your saved passengers immediately. Tickets and packages you paid for stay valid; their records are kept for accounting without the link to you.

## Send a Padala package

1. On **Padala**, choose the terminal you will drop the package at and where the recipient claims it (only pairs a bus serves are offered).
2. Enter sender, recipient, what is inside, weight and declared value. The fee is shown as you type: base + per kilogram (rounded up) + valuation % of the declared value.
3. Pay on PayMongo. You get a tracking reference **PD…**.
4. Drop the package off within 3 days. Staff update its status: received, on the bus, ready for pick-up, claimed.
5. Anyone with the reference can follow it at **Track a package**; the public page shows only first names and last initials.
